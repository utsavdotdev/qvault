## Chapter 1: Introduction
* What is software crisis? Justify this statement "Software doesn't wear out". Explain how modern software engineering practices helped overcome it.
* What are the characteristics of good software? Distinguish between external and internal software qualities along with examples.
* Differentiate between correctness, reliability, and robustness of a software.
* Define software. List the typical software characteristics.
* What factors have contributed to the making of the present software crisis? Suggest the possible solutions.
* Give a suitable definition of software quality and briefly describe the rationale for your definition. Explain with quality attributes for software.
* If the principal jobs of Software Engineering are to write codes and programs then why do such engineering need CASE tools? What are the benefits of using CASE tools?

## Chapter 2: The Software Process
* Explain the spiral model of software development. Why is it preferred in high-risk projects?
* Explain fundamental activities of the software process. 
* Explain the Incremental Model for software development with its pros and cons. How do you implement it?
* In what types of project, the prototype process model can be used? Explain with an example. What are the risks if prototyping becomes uncontrolled?
* What is Software Development Life Cycle? Discuss the various stages of the waterfall process model. What are its demerits? Why is it not an accurate reflection of software development activities?
* Define Software Process Model. Differentiate Spiral model and Incremental model highlighting their advantages and disadvantages.
* Explain how both the waterfall model and prototyping model of software process can be accommodated in spiral process model.
* Explain the V-model for software development process.
* Describe the evolutionary model, in brief, explaining how it reduces crisis problem.
* What are the reasons for software runways?
* Describe the main activities in the software design process and the output of these activities.

## Chapter 3: Software Requirements Engineering
* Why do we need to perform requirement analysis and refinement during requirement engineering process?
* Why do we need to do a feasibility study before accepting any project? Explain feasibility and its types. What are the major components of a feasibility study report?
* Differentiate between functional and non-functional requirements with examples.
* What is the importance of an SRS document in software development? Why does an engineer ensure that functional and non-functional needs are in it?
* Why is it so difficult to gain a clear understanding of what the customer wants? Describe the guidelines for the requirement elicitation process with suitable examples.
* Requirements engineering is the process of identifying, eliciting, analyzing, specifying, validating and managing... Justify with examples.
* Distinguish between user and system requirements.
* Prepare functional and non-functional requirements based on given scenarios (e.g., Movie theatre, Airlines reservation system, Online food ordering, Telemedicine project, Restaurant, Ride sharing, Time Management System).
* Compare: Consistency vs. completeness in requirements engineering.
